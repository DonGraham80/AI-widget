AI Form Agent - Multi-Form Workflow Demo

How it works:

This demo shows the AI form agent handling a multi-step business registration workflow with:

  • ✅ Multiple Steps: Basic Info → Ownership Details → Operations
  • ✅ Progress Indicator: Shows current step (e.g., "Step 2 of 3")
  • ✅ Conditional Logic: Ownership step only shows for Partnership, Corporation, or LLC
  • ✅ Data Passing: Information from previous steps is available to the AI
  • ✅ Single Submission: All data submitted together at the end

Try saying: "I want to register my tech company called InnovateTech as an LLC"
